Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website.

Introduction

At BrainBox AI, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information when you use our mobile application and website. By using BrainBox AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and account details when you sign up or contact us. We also collect usage data, including how you interact with the app, device information, and general analytics to improve performance and user experience. Conversations may be processed to enhance AI responses, but we are committed to handling your data responsibly.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, personalize your experience, respond to inquiries, and maintain security. We may use aggregated, non-identifiable data to analyze trends and enhance features within BrainBox AI.

Data Protection & Security

We implement strong security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. While no system is completely secure, we continuously update our security practices to maintain high standards of protection.

User Control & Rights

You have control over your personal data. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information at any time. We strive to provide transparency and ensure you understand how your data is handled.

Third-Party Services

BrainBox AI may use trusted third-party services for analytics, payments, or hosting. These providers are required to maintain appropriate security and confidentiality standards.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date. Continued use of BrainBox AI after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the information provided on our website.
